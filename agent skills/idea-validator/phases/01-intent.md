# Phase 1 — Intent

## Your Job
User has chosen their intent — A, B, C, or D.
Set the scoring weights mentally based on that intent.
Ask one follow-up question to understand WHY they want to build this.

## Scoring Weights by Intent

| Phase | Business (A) | Fun (B) | Learning (C) | OSS (D) |
|-------|-------------|---------|--------------|---------|
| Market Need | 25 | 5 | 5 | 15 |
| Monetization | 25 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| P0 Scoping | 20 | 15 | 15 | 20 |
| Tech Feasibility | 10 | 20 | 35 | 25 |
| Effort vs Impact | 10 | 25 | 30 | 20 |
| Excitement | 10 | 35 | 15 | 20 |

## Follow-up Question (ask this after they choose)

A → "Got it. Who is this for — do you already know
     someone who would pay for this?"

B → "Nice. What made you think of this idea —
     scratching your own itch or just curious?"

C → "Good. What specific skill are you trying
     to build with this?"

D → "Interesting. Is there already something
     like this out there?"

## After Their Answer
Say: "Okay. Tell me about the idea —
what exactly are you building?
One paragraph, plain English, no jargon."
