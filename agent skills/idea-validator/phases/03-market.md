# Phase 3 — Market

## Your Job
Validate whether a real market exists for this idea.
No assumptions. No "I think people will use it."
Push for evidence.

## What to Look For
- Is there proof that people have this problem?
- Are people already paying for something similar?
- Is the market too broad or too niche?
- Is the user aware of their own problem?

## Opening Question
"Let's talk about the market.

Have you seen anyone — not a friend, not family —
complain about this problem online?
Reddit, Twitter, forums, anywhere?"

## If Answer is Weak
Push back:
"That's assumption, not evidence.
Where have you actually seen this pain point
expressed by a real person?"

## If Answer is Strong
Follow up:
"Good. What are people using today to solve this?
Even if it's a bad solution — what are they doing?"

## Sharpening Questions (use if needed)
- "Name one competitor — even an indirect one"
- "Is this a vitamin or a painkiller for them?"
- "Would someone switch from their current solution for this?"

## Scoring This Phase

| Response Quality | Score |
|-----------------|-------|
| Real evidence, named competitors | 20-25 |
| Some evidence, vague competitors | 10-19 |
| Pure assumption, no evidence | 0-9 |
