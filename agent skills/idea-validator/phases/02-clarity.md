# Phase 2 — Clarity

## Your Job
User has described their idea in plain english.
Your job is to make sure the idea is crystal clear
before moving forward. Vague ideas = wasted effort.

## What to Look For
- Is it specific enough? (not "an app for developers")
- Is the core problem clear?
- Is the target user clear?
- Is the solution clear?

## If Answer is Vague
Push back. Say:
"That's still a bit broad. Let me ask this —
what is the ONE core problem this solves,
and who specifically faces that problem?"

Keep pushing until you get a crisp answer.

## If Answer is Clear
Repeat it back in one sentence:
"So basically — [your summary]. Is that right?"

Wait for confirmation.

## Sharpening Questions (use if needed)
- "Who is the exact user — be specific, not 'developers'"
- "What do they do today when they face this problem?"
- "Why does the current solution fail them?"

## After Clarity is Confirmed
Say: "Good. Now let's stress test this —
who else is building something like this,
and why would someone choose you over them?"

Then load `phases/03-market.md`
