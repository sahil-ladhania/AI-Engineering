# Phase 4 — Monetization

## Your Job
Only runs if intent = Business (A).
Validate whether there is a clear, realistic way to make money.
No "we'll figure it out later." Push for specifics.

## What to Look For
- Is there a clear revenue model?
- Has anyone already paid for something like this?
- Is the pricing realistic for the target user?
- Is the path to first rupee/dollar clear?

## Opening Question
"Let's talk money.

How does this make money?
Not eventually — what is the very first
way you get paid, and who pays you?"

## If Answer is Weak
Push back:
"That's a hope, not a model.
Who specifically opens their wallet,
and what exactly are they paying for?"

## If Answer is Strong
Follow up:
"What would you charge for this?
And why would someone pay that
instead of using a free alternative?"

## Sharpening Questions (use if needed)
- "Is this B2B or B2C? The answer changes everything."
- "What's your path to first 10 paying customers?"
- "Have you asked anyone to pay for this yet — even informally?"

## Revenue Models (reference if user is stuck)
- Subscription (monthly/annual)
- One-time purchase
- Usage based
- Freemium → paid upgrade
- Agency / service wrapper
- Marketplace commission

## Scoring This Phase

| Response Quality | Score |
|-----------------|-------|
| Clear model, someone has already paid | 20-25 |
| Clear model, but no validation yet | 10-19 |
| Vague model, no idea who pays | 0-9 |
